# Privacy Policy for Docs&Loyalty

Effective date: 11 Sep 2026

Docs&Loyalty is provided by Marko Benkek (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## 1. Overview

Docs&Loyalty lets you store and organize images of documents and loyalty cards on your device.

The app does not require an account and does not automatically upload your documents or card images to developer-operated servers.

The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores information you add or generate while using its features, including:

- Document and loyalty-card images, including optional back-side images.
- Item titles and document categories.
- Optional store website URLs and downloaded store icons.
- Item identifiers, creation dates, and your preferred item order.
- App preferences, such as the loyalty list or grid display mode.

This information is used to display, search, organize, export, and share your items.

Image cropping and PDF generation take place on your device.

## 3. Camera and Photo Selection

The app requests camera permission when you choose to photograph an item.

When you import an existing image, the app uses the system photo picker to access the image you select. It does not require unrestricted access to your entire photo library.

You can manage camera permission in iOS Settings. Denying camera access prevents camera capture but does not prevent importing an image through the photo picker.

## 4. Optional Store Icon Downloads

Providing a website URL for a loyalty item is optional.

If you provide one, the app may contact that website and any icon-hosting services referenced by it to retrieve a store icon. Missing icons may be requested again later.

These requests disclose ordinary network information to the contacted services, including your IP address, the requested URL, and request headers. Those services may process or retain this information according to their own privacy policies.

The icon downloader uses HTTPS and an ephemeral network session without persistent cookie storage.

Document images, loyalty-card images, and item titles are not included in icon-download requests.

Leave the website field blank if you do not want the app to request an icon for that item.

## 5. Sharing and Collection Cloning

Sharing happens when you initiate it.

### Individual item sharing

The app creates a PDF of the selected item's images and makes it available through the standard iOS share sheet.

The app or destination you choose receives the exported file and handles it according to its own privacy practices.

### Collection cloning

The clone feature exports all items from all modules into a transferable archive. This includes stored images and item metadata, such as titles, categories, and any saved store URLs and icons.

The archive is not limited to the current tab or search results.

You choose how and with whom to share it, including through AirDrop where available.

Shared PDFs and archives may contain sensitive information. Review the contents and recipient before sharing. Deleting an item in Docs&Loyalty does not delete copies already shared or imported on another device.

## 6. Device Storage and Backups

Your collection is stored in the app's local storage. The app also keeps a local metadata recovery copy.

Docs&Loyalty does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. However, app data may be included in iCloud or computer-based device backups, depending on your Apple settings.

These backups are managed outside the app. We do not operate or control them.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Items remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app and its data.

You can delete individual items using the deletion controls. This removes them from the collection and deletes their associated local image and icon files.

Deleting an imported item does not delete the original image from your photo library.

To remove the app's local data entirely, use **Delete App** in iOS rather than **Offload App**, which can retain app data.

Temporary files may be created while generating PDFs or importing and exporting archives.

Copies in device backups, exported files, other apps, or recipients' devices are not removed when you delete an item locally. Manage those copies separately through the relevant device, service, or recipient.

We cannot remotely access, retrieve, or delete the collection stored on your device.

## 8. Advertising and Tracking

The current app does not include advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use an advertising identifier to track you.

We do not sell your stored documents or loyalty-card information.

Apple may separately process App Store, device, or diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

## 9. Security

The app uses the storage isolation and security mechanisms provided by iOS. Optional icon downloads use HTTPS.

Exported PDFs and collection archives are not password-protected by the app. Anyone who obtains a readable copy may be able to access its contents.

Protect your device with a passcode, keep iOS updated, and share sensitive files only with trusted recipients. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your Choices

You can:

- Choose which images and details to add.
- Omit store URLs to avoid optional icon requests.
- Manage camera permission in iOS Settings.
- Export individual items or your collection.
- Delete stored items or delete the app and its data.
- Manage device backups through Apple's settings and tools.
